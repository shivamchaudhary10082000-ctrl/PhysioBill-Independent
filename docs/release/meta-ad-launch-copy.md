# PhysioBill Meta Ad Launch Copy — Conservative Template

Status: candidate copy only. Re-check the live Meta Advertising Standards in Ads Manager immediately before campaign submission.

## Recommended first campaign

Objective: traffic or landing-page views to the public PhysioBill discovery page.

Primary text:

> PhysioBill helps people find physiotherapy professionals offering home visits, clinic visits and telephysiotherapy. Search by city and service mode, review patient-safe professional profiles, and request an appointment.

Headline:

> Find physiotherapy care near you

Description:

> Verified professional profiles • Broad service areas • Appointment requests

CTA:

> Learn More

Landing page:

> Canonical PhysioBill public landing/discovery page only. Do not send ad traffic directly into a private patient, professional or Admin route.

## Do not submit copy like this

- “Are you suffering from back pain?”
- “Is your knee pain getting worse?”
- “Stroke patient at home? Book now.”
- “100% relief guaranteed.”
- “Permanent cure in 7 days.”
- “Best / No. 1 physiotherapist in Surat.”
- “We know you need physiotherapy.”
- “Before / after” transformation claims presented as a promised clinical result.

These examples either imply a sensitive health attribute about the viewer or make an unsupported/guaranteed clinical claim.

## Creative rules for the first campaign

Prefer:

- neutral professional or rehabilitation-service visuals;
- app/discovery interface imagery that contains no patient records;
- physiotherapy equipment or non-identifiable service imagery;
- plain service descriptions and broad geography.

Avoid:

- patient faces or treatment footage without specific public-use permission;
- visible clinical notes, prescriptions, invoices, phone numbers or patient IDs;
- shocking injury, pain or disability imagery;
- staged testimonials presented as real patients;
- before/after treatment transformations;
- fake star ratings or fabricated review counts.

## Tracking rule

The first launch should run without Meta Pixel / Conversions API inside PhysioBill.

Do not send diagnosis, condition, appointment reason, therapist clinical notes, invoice details, PAT/PHY identifiers, phone numbers or other patient/clinical fields to Meta through:

- event names;
- URL parameters / UTMs containing patient information;
- custom event properties;
- custom audiences;
- server-side conversion payloads.

If advertising measurement is added later, it needs a separate privacy/security review and data-minimization design before code is changed.

## Campaign approval gate

Before pressing Publish in Meta Ads Manager:

1. compare the final ad copy and image/video against the current Meta Advertising Standards;
2. verify the destination URL loads without authentication and has Privacy / Terms / Professional Standards links;
3. confirm no Meta tracking code is present unless separately approved;
4. confirm the landing page makes no guaranteed outcome or government-verification claim;
5. confirm the first public professional profile has genuine, current credential/registration facts;
6. take screenshots of the final submitted copy/creative and store them with the launch record.
