# PhysioBill Product Roadmap

## Locked future phase — Therapist Discovery & Patient Booking

This phase is intentionally **not part of Phase 4 Slice 2**.

Future requirements:
- Physiotherapists can create and manage a professional profile.
- Physiotherapists can optionally make themselves discoverable by location and service area.
- Patients can register and search for physiotherapists in their area.
- Patients can view therapist profiles and future availability.
- Patients can request or book appointments and interact through the platform.
- Accepted/completed appointments should eventually feed into the existing production chain: Patient → Visit → Clinical Record → Invoice.

This phase must not weaken physiotherapist ownership isolation or replace the existing production authority model.
