# PhysioBill Product Roadmap

This roadmap records future requirements only. Unless explicitly stated otherwise, these items are **not implemented in the current Phase 4 UX slice**.

## Future Therapist Discovery & Patient Booking

- Physiotherapists can create and manage professional profiles.
- Physiotherapists can optionally become discoverable by location and service area.
- Patients can register and search therapists in their area.
- Patients can view therapist profiles and future availability.
- Availability should distinguish Home Visit, Clinic Visit and Telephysiotherapy where supported.
- Patients can request/book appointments and interact through the platform.
- Accepted/completed appointments should eventually feed the existing production chain: Patient → Visit → Clinical Record → Invoice → Payment.
- Discovery/booking must preserve physiotherapist ownership isolation and the production authority model.

## Patient Directory & Treatment Episode Analytics

Future architecture should add an explicit authoritative treatment-episode model before monthly/yearly treatment analytics are shown. It should support:
- Patient directory search by month/year;
- treatment episode status: Ongoing / Recovered-Discharged / Left-Discontinued;
- authoritative started_at and ended_at/status history;
- monthly/yearly Patient counts;
- Visit counts;
- financial-status summaries;
- compact colored status indicators.

These values must not be guessed from the existence or absence of Visits.

## Patient Advance / Credit Ledger

Future financial architecture must support genuine patient advances without forcing overpayment onto an invoice.

Example: if a patient pays ₹1,000 and the current invoice requires ₹700, ₹700 may settle the invoice and ₹300 becomes Patient Credit / Advance Balance.

Future capabilities should include:
- advance received;
- advance balance;
- allocation to a future invoice;
- refund;
- reversal/correction;
- immutable audit history.

## Real Therapist Payment Destination

Current Cash/UPI/Bank Transfer values record payment method only. Future architecture must allow each physiotherapist to configure their own legitimate payment destination, including applicable UPI/payment details.

Patient-facing payment capability may later include Pay Now, UPI intent, UPI QR, payment links and provider verification/webhooks where appropriate. Money should belong to/go to the physiotherapist's configured destination rather than silently becoming a central PhysioBill collection account unless a later explicitly designed and legally appropriate architecture requires otherwise.

## Patient Financial Ledger

Future patient financial views should consolidate chronologically:
- invoices;
- payments;
- outstanding balances;
- advance credit;
- refunds;
- corrections/reversals.

## Professional Invoices / Receipts / Claim Documentation

Future finalized invoices, payment receipts and supporting treatment documentation should be professional PDF documents suitable for legitimate submission where applicable for private insurance/mediclaim, government health schemes, employer reimbursement and other healthcare reimbursement.

Claim acceptance must never be promised. Eligibility remains subject to patient policy/scheme, coverage, provider eligibility, clinical requirements, referral/prescription requirements, insurer/government requirements and supporting documentation. PhysioBill must never invent clinical or financial facts to improve claim acceptance.

Future documents should derive from genuine records including where applicable:
- verified physiotherapist identity;
- patient identity;
- invoice number and invoice date;
- service/treatment and condition/clinical indication where appropriate;
- session/treatment dates and number of sessions;
- fees, legitimate additional charges, discount and GST where legitimately applicable;
- total;
- payment transactions, methods and references where appropriate;
- amount paid and balance;
- service delivery mode and service location.

Invoice and Payment Receipt remain conceptually distinct documents.

## Therapist Verification & Profile Presentation

Account registration must not automatically imply Verified Physiotherapist.

Future verification may support evidence such as qualification, institution/university, professional registration/licence where applicable and other legitimate verification evidence. Sensitive uploaded verification documents must not automatically become public.

Professional/claim-oriented documents may later require an appropriately verified therapist account. Do not claim "Insurance Approved." A safer concept is "Generated by / issued through a verified physiotherapist account" only when verification has actually occurred.

Future therapist-profile enhancements should also consider:
- therapist profile picture/avatar upload;
- verified public therapist profile;
- premium patient-facing profile presentation.

## Premium Visual Design

Future visual enhancement should consider a polished premium background/canvas/hero treatment and a consistent elevated visual system across therapist and patient-facing experiences while preserving readability, accessibility and mobile performance.

## Document Integrity

Future finalized PDFs should support tamper-resistant verification such as:
- immutable document/invoice ID;
- verification reference;
- QR/verification mechanism;
- void/replacement status;
- minimal privacy-preserving verification information.

A public verification page must not expose full clinical records.

## Service Location / Anti-Fraud

Do not use a simplistic Patient State = Therapist State rule.

Future in-person/home/clinic records should distinguish actual service location, therapist/service location, patient, date/time, Visit and Clinical Record. Implausible location relationships may later be flagged for verification rather than automatically rejected.

## Telephysiotherapy

Future architecture should explicitly support telephysiotherapy. A legitimate telephysio record may include verified therapist, therapist location, patient, patient location, date, start/end time or duration, service mode = Telephysiotherapy, consent where required, Clinical Record, Invoice, Payment and Receipt.

Cross-state/cross-country telephysiotherapy must not automatically imply legal eligibility. Applicable professional, regulatory and jurisdiction rules must be respected. Reimbursement documentation must derive from the actual recorded service and must never claim reimbursement eligibility automatically.

## Communication / Business Features

Future consideration should retain:
- multilingual UI;
- WhatsApp reminders;
- SMS reminders;
- automated outstanding-payment reminders;
- configurable reminder templates;
- cloud synchronization/backup;
- offline/PWA capability without making localStorage production financial authority;
- GST and Non-GST invoicing;
- professional/customizable invoice themes;
- thermal-friendly printing where useful;
- appropriate GST/GSTR-related reporting/export where legally applicable.

Do not promise free WhatsApp/SMS delivery because provider/platform charges may apply.

Purchase/sales management and barcode scanning should not be prioritized for the core physiotherapy product unless a later genuine inventory/business requirement justifies them.
